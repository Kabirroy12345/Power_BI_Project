🎤 30-Second Interview Elevator Pitch & Architecture Summary
Ready-to-present talking points aligned with your repository & data
1. End-to-End Pipeline: Ingested 145,728 hourly machine logs across 5 lines & 20 machines (Jan–Dec 2024). Built a Pandas ETL script (etl_pipeline.py) that deduplicates records, clips negative values, enforces good + defect = total_units, and normalizes flat logs into a Kimball Star Schema (1 Fact + 5 Dimensions).
2. Advanced Power BI DAX Layer: Authored 26 DAX measures in a dedicated _Measures table—using SUMX + RELATED for weighted performance and revenue ($3.11B), DATEADD/DATESYTD for Time Intelligence, and dynamic MTBF Hours (33.0h fleet avg).
3. Actionable Statistical Findings: • OEE Gap: Plant OEE is 82.54% (Line_B leads at 85.4%; Line_E trails at 78.9%). Reaching 85% yields +610.8K units (+$90.3M/yr).
• Shift ANOVA: Night shift drops to 79.5% vs 87.3% Morning (F = 3,433.5, p < 0.0001).
• Workforce & IoT: Certifying the 3 Junior operators cuts their scrap from 5.12% → 3.12%, and alerting at >8 mm/s vibration prevents the 6.36% defect surge.
